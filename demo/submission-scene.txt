THE PRICE OF GOLD

EXT. ROCKY COVE - LATE AFTERNOON

Two adult partners, MAYA and DEV, kneel beside a weathered chest on dry
rock above the waterline. The sea fills the background. Neither enters it.

Maya lifts a small stone from the chest and closes her bare hand around it.
Her other hand brushes a discarded metal clasp. Gold spreads across it.

DEV
We found it.

Maya takes an apple from their bag. It turns gold beneath her fingers.
She presses her thumbnail against its skin. Solid metal.

MAYA
Then why am I still hungry?

Dev reaches for her hand. Maya recoils before their fingers meet.

MAYA
Don't touch me.

His hand stays suspended between them. Behind it, the sea catches the
last light. Everything they wanted is at their feet. They cannot touch.
