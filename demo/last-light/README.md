## Why this story

A storm cuts off a harbor. Elsie, a signal clerk, and Arun, a rescue courier, carry one final message to a stranded boat. The genre is grounded 1935 rescue suspense with heroic framing, not time travel or alternate-history science fiction. This distinction makes period research meaningful.

Three connected pages demonstrate three different jobs: spotting a visual period mismatch, making an evidence-backed caption correction without rewriting dialogue, and declining to identify an ambiguous symbol. Do not hardcode these expected results into the app.
